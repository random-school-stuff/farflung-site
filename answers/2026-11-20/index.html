Prompt by prompt

Prompt 1: Pin a country where Julius Caesar commanded in a battle

Gem, 100 km: Netherlands.

13 countries are accepted for this prompt: 1 Common, 3 Uncommon, 5 Rare, 3 Elusive and 1 at the top tier. The obvious pick is France, which scores 40 km — 60 km behind the gem. If your instinct was Italy, read the card literally: the prompt asks where Caesar commanded, not where he was born.

Prompt 2: Pin a country whose own entry has won the Oscar for Best International Feature Film

Gem, 100 km: Côte d'Ivoire.

The widest prompt of the day: 28 countries accepted, split 1 Common, 2 Uncommon, 10 Rare, 12 Elusive and 3 Farflung. France again sits at the bottom with 40 km. Note the wording — the country's own submission has to have won, so a film that was merely nominated does not qualify.

Prompt 3: Pin a country whose highest recorded temperature is over 50°C

Gem, 100 km: Turkmenistan.

24 countries accepted: 1 Common, 4 Uncommon, 10 Rare, 7 Elusive, 2 Farflung. Saudi Arabia is the 40 km answer most people reach for. Central Asia is where this prompt pays off.

Prompt 4: Pin a country where voting is compulsory by law

Gem, 100 km: Bulgaria.

30 countries accepted: 1 Common, 3 Uncommon, 9 Rare, 13 Elusive, 4 Farflung. Australia is the 40 km reflex answer — it is genuinely compulsory there, which is exactly why it scores least.

Prompt 5: Pin a country that has hosted a Formula One World Championship race

Gem, 100 km: Morocco.

36 countries accepted — the most generous prompt of the expedition: 1 Common, 2 Uncommon, 14 Rare, 15 Elusive, 4 Farflung. Monaco at 40 km is almost a joke answer. Morocco hosted once, in 1958.

What each tier is worth

Tiers pay Common 40 km, Uncommon 55 km, Rare 70 km, Elusive 85 km and Farflung 100 km, so five gems make 500 km. Tiers are judged per prompt, not per country: the same country can be Farflung on one card and Common on another.
