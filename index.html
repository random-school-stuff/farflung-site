Farflung Answers Today: Expedition 47

Farflung is a free daily geography game: five prompts, one country pinned for each, and the rarer your answer the further it carries you. This page lists the gem answer for every prompt in today's expedition, the number of countries accepted in each rarity tier, and the previous day's answers — because a new expedition opens at midnight in your own time zone, so which day you are on depends on where you are.

Game date 2026-11-20. If it is still 19 November where you live, you are on Expedition #46 — its answers are further down this page.

Prompt 1: Pin a country where Julius Caesar commanded in a battle

Gem, 100 km: Netherlands.

13 countries are accepted for this prompt: 1 Common, 3 Uncommon, 5 Rare, 3 Elusive and 1 at the top tier. The obvious pick is France, which scores 40 km — 60 km behind the gem. If your instinct was Italy, read the card literally: the prompt asks where Caesar commanded, not where he was born.

Prompt 3: Pin a country whose highest recorded temperature is over 50°C

Gem, 100 km: Turkmenistan.

24 countries accepted: 1 Common, 4 Uncommon, 10 Rare, 7 Elusive, 2 Farflung. Saudi Arabia is the 40 km answer most people reach for. Central Asia is where this prompt pays off, and Turkmenistan's record high is the reason it sits at the top.

Prompt 4: Pin a country where voting is compulsory by law

Gem, 100 km: Bulgaria.

30 countries accepted: 1 Common, 3 Uncommon, 9 Rare, 13 Elusive, 4 Farflung. Australia is the 40 km reflex answer — it is genuinely compulsory there, which is exactly why it scores least. Enforcement is what separates the tiers: a country that writes the rule into law but never fines anyone still counts.

How the five tiers score

A correct pin adds kilometres, and how many depends on how rare your country is. The tiers run Common 40 km, Uncommon 55 km, Rare 70 km, Elusive 85 km and Farflung 100 km, so a perfect five-gem expedition is 500 km. A pin that does not fit the prompt scores zero. These figures come from the stored daily expedition record; the official how-to-play page names the five tiers but does not publish the kilometre values.

The tiers are judged per prompt, not per country — the same country can be Farflung on one card and Common on another. So there is no list of "rare countries" to memorise. The only way to score well is to read each card literally and pick the country that fits it in the least expected way.

Official example, not from the daily game: for "a country where German is an official language", Germany is Common, Austria Uncommon, Liechtenstein Rare, Belgium Elusive and Luxembourg Farflung, because German is co-official there alongside French and Luxembourgish.

Three things that trip people up

  • Territories count for their parent country. Greenland scores as Denmark, Guam scores as the United States. Pinning an overseas territory is not a shortcut to a rare answer.
  • One attempt per expedition. Leaving mid-round is allowed and you can come back, but with the 60-second clock on, the timer keeps running while you are away and a timed-out prompt scores nothing.
  • 197 countries are in play — 193 UN members plus Vatican City, Palestine, Kosovo and Taiwan. Every prompt has at least one gem sitting at the top tier.

Questions

What are today's Farflung answers?

Expedition #47 on 20 November 2026 has Netherlands, Côte d'Ivoire, Turkmenistan, Bulgaria and Morocco as its selected 100 km gems, in prompt order. The full accepted list stays inside the game; these are the top-tier picks for each card.

When does a new Farflung expedition open?

At midnight in your own local time. Everyone gets the same five prompts for a given date, so two players in different time zones can be on different expedition numbers at the same moment — which is why this page keeps the previous day up as well.

Can I play today's expedition a second time?

No. One attempt per daily expedition. Practice mode gives you a single repeated prompt with no clock, so you can learn the map controls without touching your daily score.

Do I need an account to play Farflung?

No. Farflung runs free in a browser on a phone or a computer, as a guest, with nothing to download.

Is there a Farflung unlimited mode?

Not on the official game. The developer's FAQ says themed packs come first and an unlimited mode follows later. You can replay the past week from the in-game menu.

Is farflunganswer.com the official Farflung site?

No. This is an independent fan site tracking the daily answers. The official game is published by Farflung LLC at farflung.game.
