Farflung Answers Today

Farflung is a free daily geography game: five prompts, one country pinned for each, and the rarer your answer the further it takes you. This page collects today's expedition, explains how the rarities are scored and gives the context you need to finish a round without spoiling it for yourself.

Today's expedition

Expedition 47 · Friday 20 November 2026. Everyone playing on this date gets the same five prompts. A new expedition opens at midnight in your local time, so if you are reading this from another time zone the set may already have rolled over.

  1. Pin a country where Julius Caesar commanded in a battle.
  2. Pin a country whose own film has won the Oscar for Best International Feature Film.
  3. Pin a country whose highest recorded temperature is over 50°C.
  4. Pin a country where voting is compulsory by law.
  5. Pin a country that has hosted a Formula One World Championship race.

Each prompt accepts many countries, so there is no single right answer — there is a spread from obvious to unexpected, and the unexpected ones travel furthest. The gem answer for each prompt is added here once today's round is done, so the page stays useful for players who come back the next morning.

How far an answer takes you

A correct answer adds distance in kilometres. How much depends on how rare your choice is, in five steps: Common is the answer most people think of first, then Uncommon, Rare, Elusive, and finally Farflung, the top rarity that goes furthest of all. An answer that does not fit the prompt is a miss and adds nothing. Every prompt has at least one gem — a genuinely correct answer that almost nobody thinks of, sitting at the top rarity.

A worked example from the official guide, not from the daily game: for "a country where German is an official language", Germany is Common, Austria is Uncommon, Liechtenstein is Rare, Belgium is Elusive and Luxembourg is Farflung, because German shares official status there with French and Luxembourgish.

Three things that trip people up

  • Territories count for their parent country. A pin on Greenland scores as Denmark, and a pin on Guam scores as the United States.
  • You get one attempt per day. Leaving mid-round is fine, you can come back and continue. But if you play with the 60-second clock on, the timer keeps running while you are away, and a prompt that times out scores nothing.
  • The map hides nothing, but the difficulty settings do. You can turn off the country name under your pin and turn off borders if the default feels too generous. Practice rounds are unlimited and do not use up your daily game.

Questions

When does a new Farflung expedition open?

At midnight in your own local time. Everyone gets the same five prompts for a given date, so players in different time zones may be on different expeditions during the same hour.

Can I play today's expedition a second time?

No. You get one attempt per daily expedition. If you want another round without affecting your daily score, use the practice round, which has a single prompt, no clock and no limit.

Do I need an account to play Farflung?

No. Farflung plays in a browser on a phone or a computer, free, as a guest, with nothing to download.

Is there a Farflung unlimited mode?

Not yet. The developer says themed packs will come first and an unlimited mode will follow later.

Is farflunganswer.com the official Farflung site?

No. This is an independent fan site that tracks the daily answers. The official game is published by Farflung LLC at farflung.game.
